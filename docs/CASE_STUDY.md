# Operational visibility for a fictional apparel retailer

## Business question

A retail team needs to see which orders are late, which suppliers are overloaded,
where costs exceed targets, and which development milestones require follow-up.
This portfolio models that situation with invented data, rather than presenting
a real client's results.

## Demonstrated work

1. Generate relational supplier, order, costing, milestone, and schedule inputs.
2. Validate quantities, references, costs, and dates before building a warehouse.
3. Define understandable delivery and capacity metrics with explicit denominators.
4. Present eight dashboard views with consistent filters and downloadable detail.
5. Test the complete dashboard flow and document the data's origin.

## How to demo it to a client

Start with Overview and review the overdue-order table. Filter to one brand, then
open Delivery to compare suppliers. Open Capacity to locate utilization above
100%, and Costing to inspect above-target orders. Use Cost simulator to change
component costs, then open Production schedule to review line overlaps. Finish
with Milestones and Supplier intelligence to connect execution with allocation.

Explain that the figures are simulated, that integrations would be scoped to
the client's sources, and that their metric definitions need agreement before
implementation. No revenue improvement, savings, productivity gain, or deployment
result is claimed from this fictional exercise.
